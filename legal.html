About Us

LoadLink Pakistan is a truck and load marketplace that helps customers post freight requirements and transporters discover suitable loads and bookings.

Terms & Conditions

By creating an account or using LoadLink Pakistan, you agree to use the service lawfully and provide accurate information.

  • Users are responsible for the accuracy of load, vehicle, contact and identity information they submit.
  • Transporters must provide valid vehicle and driving documents and must comply with applicable Pakistani transport and road-safety requirements.
  • Bookings, fares and trip details should be confirmed by the parties before a trip starts.
  • LoadLink Pakistan may suspend accounts for fraud, abuse, misleading information, unsafe activity, or repeated policy violations.
  • The platform facilitates connections and booking workflows; users remain responsible for the legality and physical handling of their goods and vehicles.
  • Do not upload passwords, payment-card secrets, or unnecessary sensitive information.

Privacy Policy

We use account information, contact details, booking/load data and operational information to provide the marketplace, authentication, notifications, payments and support.

  • Passwords are stored as secure password hashes, not plain text.
  • Mobile OTP verification is used to confirm control of a registered phone number.
  • Location data may be processed during live trip tracking when the relevant feature is enabled.
  • Information may be shared with service providers needed to deliver communications, hosting, maps, payments or notifications.
  • We do not ask users to send OTPs or passwords to support staff.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Cancellation and refund outcomes depend on the booking/payment status and the terms agreed between the customer and transporter. Where a payment provider has processed a transaction, provider rules may also apply.

  • Before a trip is accepted, a booking may generally be cancelled without a platform service charge unless a specific charge is shown at checkout.
  • After acceptance or trip commencement, refunds may require review of the booking and trip circumstances.
  • For disputed payments, contact support with the booking/trip reference and payment reference.

Help & Support

For login, OTP, booking, payment or trip issues, keep your booking/trip ID and registered mobile number ready. Never share your password or OTP with another person.
